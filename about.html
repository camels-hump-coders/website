About Our Team

Middle School Robotics Team from Huntington, Vermont

Meet Our Team

We are a team of middle & high school students from Huntington, Vermont who compeate in FIRST® Tech Challenge.

Our Story

We are a group of middle & high school students from Huntington, Vermont who share a passion for building, creating, and inventing. Our team formed recently when one experienced teammate brought together students with a shared interest in technology and innovation.

"We all love to build, create and invent." - This simple statement captures who we are and what drives us forward.

As participants in the FIRST® Tech Challenge, we engage in:

  • Science and technology exploration
  • Building and programming robots
  • Coding the robot to solve challenges
  • Engaging in community outreach

What We Do

Robot Design

We design, build, and program robots using a reusable kit to complete missions on a themed playing field. tthe kit contains most of the components needed for building and programming. However parts can also be manufactured.

Robot Competition

At each event, we showcase our robot's capabilities and compete against other teams. The robot is both autonomously and manually controlled.

Core Values

We embody the FIRST® Core Values of discovery, innovation, impact, inclusion, teamwork, and fun! As well as gracious professionalism which means we treat each other with respect.
